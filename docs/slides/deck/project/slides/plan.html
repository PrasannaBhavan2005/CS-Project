What frame-aware MPQUIC teaches us for live video

1. Split frames, don't pin

Whole-frame pinning (F1) leaves capacity idle. Completion-synchronized byte splitting (F2) achieves near-ideal latency under sustained load.

2. Rate estimation is critical

Comparing F2 vs Oracle R2 shows delivery-rate estimation under app-limited bursts is the primary bottleneck—not the split formula itself.

3. Guard PTO rescue

Aggressive 1-PTO rescue recovers isolated stalls but triggers congestion cascades during multi-second cellular blackouts; needs hysteresis.

Streaming a live lecture over two weak internet connections

Send whole video frames, not scattered packets, over two paths at once

Multipath QUIC · emulated cellular links · 2 November 2026

One sentence: a teacher streaming a live lecture from a phone often does not have one connection good enough, so we use two at once, and we test whether sending each video frame as a whole unit beats the usual packet-by-packet splitting.

Sources: Zoom bandwidth requirements; Opensignal India, Feb 2026: 56–69% of tests on the three private operators met its bar

The need: a camera feed of a teacher at a board needs about 2.6 Mbps of upload, all the time. Average mobile uploads in India are 6 to 8 Mbps, but averages hide dips: a third or more of tests miss Opensignal's consistent-quality bar. When the video needs more than one link gives, frames arrive late and the picture freezes. Many teachers have a second link (WiFi plus mobile data, or a second phone), so the question is how to use both together well.
